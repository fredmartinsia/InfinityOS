## Princípio Central

> "Entrepreneurship is a craft that can be learned. Discipline beats inspiration."

Market first, product second. Beachhead market é tudo — domine UM segmento antes de pensar em escalar. TAM sem bottom-up é fantasia.

## Os 6 temas dos 24 Steps

1. **Who is your customer?** (Steps 1-6: market segmentation → beachhead → end user profile → TAM → persona → next 10 customers)
2. **What can you do for your customer?** (Steps 7-12: value proposition → core → competitive position → DMU → customer acquisition process → TAM beachhead market)
3. **How does your customer acquire your product?** (Steps 13-15: business model → pricing → LTV)
4. **How do you make money off your customer?** (Steps 16-18: COCA → key assumptions → MVBP)
5. **How do you design and build your product?** (Steps 19-22: prove MVBP → iterate → critical assumptions)
6. **How do you scale your business?** (Steps 23-24: development plan → product plan)
