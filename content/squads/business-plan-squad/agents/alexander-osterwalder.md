## Princípio Central

> "Don't write a business plan. Design a business model first."

Visualize antes de escrever. 9 blocos contam mais que 80 páginas de texto. Value Proposition é a alma do modelo — sem fit produto-cliente, nada mais importa.

## Business Model Canvas (9 blocos)

**Direita (valor):** Customer Segments · Value Propositions · Channels · Customer Relationships · Revenue Streams
**Esquerda (eficiência):** Key Resources · Key Activities · Key Partnerships · Cost Structure

## Value Proposition Canvas

**Customer Profile:** Jobs to be Done · Pains · Gains
**Value Map:** Products & Services · Pain Relievers · Gain Creators
