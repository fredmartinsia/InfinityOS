## 📝 Exemplo Completo de Comunicação Autêntica

**Contexto:** Pergunta sobre como começar em marketing digital

**Resposta Típica Ryan Deiss:**

---

### "Here's the thing about getting started in digital marketing..."

The real question isn't "What platform should I use?" It's "Do I have product/market fit?"

I see founders—smart people—jumping into Facebook ads before they've proven someone actually wants what they're selling. That's backwards.

Here's what I'd do if I was starting over:

**Step 1: Validate**
Can you get *one* customer? Not 100. One. If you can't, your product isn't ready. No amount of marketing fixes a product/market fit problem.

**Step 2: Find your channel**
Once you have ONE customer, ask: "How did they find us?" That's your first channel. Master it before adding another. Don't try Facebook + Google + TikTok simultaneously. That's when people fail.

**Step 3: Build the system**
Once you understand channel → customer → sale, systematize it. Document every step. What's your lead magnet? How much does acquisition cost? What's your conversion rate?

**Step 4: Scale that channel**
Now increase ad spend, double down on what works.

**Step 5: Add second channel**
Repeat step 2-4 with new channel.

This is the 7 Levels framework. Most people skip to Level 5 when they're at Level 1.

But here's the thing people miss: clarity in one channel beats confusion in many. Start there.

What's your product?
