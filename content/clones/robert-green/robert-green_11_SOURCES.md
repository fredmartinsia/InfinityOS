## Fontes primárias (escrita e fala diretas de Robert Green)

### Colunas Cadalyst (blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement)
1. "The Constants in CAD Management" (500ª coluna): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/the-constants-in-cad-management . Confiança ALTA. Primária direta. Framework Unified Theory, citação "as software has changed, the personas of great CAD managers haven't changed a bit".
2. "Stagflation and Low ROI? CAD Software" (out/2024): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/stagflation-and-low-roi-cad-software . ALTA. Tese de stagflation, "ROI is all about savings, no savings means no ROI", "dead cost".
3. "Drive Workflows to Gain CAD Standards Compliance" (set/2024): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/drive-workflows-to-gain-cad-standards-compliance . ALTA. "Standards are really workflows", path of least resistance, built-in compliance.
4. "Build an Efficient CAD Team" (jul/2024): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/build-an-efficient-cad-team . ALTA. Reflect annoyance method, caso Larry e Allan, "$2,860 because Allan refuses to properly format".
5. "CAD Problems? Incremental Innovation Wins Over Radical Change" (jun/2024): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/cad-problems-incremental-innovation-wins-over-radical-change . ALTA. Incremental vs radical, big costs/workflow changes/unknown disruptions.
6. Arquivo de autor: https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/author/robert-green e /page/3 . ALTA. Index de colunas.
7. Tag CAD management (inclui "How to Audit Your Office CAD Use"): https://blog.cadalyst.com/cadmanagement/tag/cad-management/page/6 . ALTA.
8. Hub Cadalyst CAD Management Resource Center: https://info.cadalyst.com/cad-management-resource-center . ALTA.

## Mentores intelectuais (não pessoas de contato, mas referências canônicas que Green cita)

32. W. Edwards Deming e a teoria geral de process control. A citação "If you can't describe what you're doing as a process then you don't know what you're doing" foi coletada na fonte 15 (SlideShare de Green), que a atribui a Deming. A obra primária de Deming onde ela aparece não foi verificada nesta coleta: usar sempre como "Deming, citado por Green". ALTA para o uso que Green faz dela, não verificada como fonte primária de Deming.
33. Toyota Production System (Andon cord, Kaizen, Taiichi Ohno). Green importa diretamente. Referência canônica, ALTA no contexto.
34. Fishman e Sullivan (origem do método Reflect Annoyance). Green cita nominalmente em "Build an Efficient CAD Team" (Cadalyst, jul/2024). ALTA no contexto.
