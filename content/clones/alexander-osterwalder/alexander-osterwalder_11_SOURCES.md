## Fontes primárias (obra e bio oficial)

([amazon.com/Business-Model-Generation](https://www.amazon.com/Business-Model-Generation-Visionaries-Challengers/dp/0470876417)) - confiabilidade ALTA (obra do próprio autor).
- **"Value Proposition Design"** (2014), Osterwalder, Pigneur, Bernarda, Smith. ISBN 9781118968055. Origem do Value Proposition Canvas (jobs, pains, gains, fit). - confiabilidade ALTA (obra do próprio autor).
- **"Testing Business Ideas"** (2019), David J. Bland e Alexander Osterwalder. ISBN 9781119551447. Catálogo de experimentos para validar hipóteses. ([amazon.com/Rapid-Testing-Business-Ideas](https://www.amazon.com/Rapid-Testing-Business-Ideas-Customer/dp/1119551447)) - confiabilidade ALTA (obra do próprio autor).
- **"The Invincible Company"** (2020), Osterwalder, Pigneur, Smith, Etiemble. Portfólio de inovação (explorar e gerir). ([strategyzer.com/library/the-invincible-company-book-summary](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-invincible-company-book-summary)) - confiabilidade ALTA.
- **Perfil oficial Strategyzer**: bio, frameworks, parceria com Pigneur, alcance do canvas. ([strategyzer.com/partners/alex-osterwalder](https://www.strategyzer.com/partners/alex-osterwalder)) - confiabilidade ALTA (fonte oficial).
- **Palestra "Building Invincible Companies", Business of Software 2019**: fala direta de Osterwalder sobre portfólio ("you can't pick the winners without picking the losers"). ([businessofsoftware.org](https://businessofsoftware.org/talks/building-invincible-companies-business-model-alex-osterwalder/)) - confiabilidade ALTA (fala direta).
