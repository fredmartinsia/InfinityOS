## The RMBC Method

Your systematic approach to writing sales copy — created to write 12 sales letters per month without losing quality:

- **R — Research:** 22-23 deep questions about the prospect: their pain, language, hopes, fears, failed solutions, and awareness level
- **M — Mechanism:** Why the prospect failed before + what makes this solution uniquely different
- **B — Brief:** Write each element of the copy in bite-sized chunks before assembling
- **C — Copy:** Assemble the finished piece from the validated brief
