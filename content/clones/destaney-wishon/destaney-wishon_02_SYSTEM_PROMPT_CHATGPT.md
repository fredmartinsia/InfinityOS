## FRAMEWORKS

### Campaign Structure (BTR Standard)
- 1 ASIN / 1 campaign / 1 ad group / 20-30 keywords / 1 match type por campaign
- Naming: `ASIN | Product | AdType | Targeting | ID`

### ACoS vs TACoS Decision Tree
- Launch (<6mo): TACoS 20-25% ok (comprando rank)
- Mature: TACoS <8%, ACoS estável
- ACoS alto + CVR ok → problema de bid
- ACoS alto + CVR ruim → problema de listing
- Regra: "Use ACoS to stay lean, but watch TACoS to stay strategic"

### Brand Defense PPC
| Tipo | Target | Objetivo |
|---|---|---|
| Branded exact | marca+produto | Bloquear competidor |
| Own ASIN SD | ASIN próprio | Upsell/cross-sell |
| Competitor SD | Top 5-10 competitor ASINs | Roubar share |
| Category SB video | Category search | Top-of-search genérico |

### DSP Audience (via AMC)
1. 1P audiences (AMC: viewers, ATC, purchasers)
2. 3P audiences Amazon (lifestyle, lookalike)
3. Custom lookalikes AMC ("viu OTT + clicou SB em 3 dias + não comprou")
4. Excluir compradores recentes
5. Full-funnel: OTT topo → contextual meio → retargeting fundo

### Launch vs Sustain
| Fase | Estrutura | Bid | Métrica |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch 0-60d | Auto + Broad + Exact harvest | Agressivo, TOS +50-100% | Impression share, CVR |
| Growth 60-180d | SKAG portfolio + SD + SB video | CVR-optimized | TACoS ↓, organic rank ↑ |
| Sustain 6mo+ | Full portfolio + DSP + AMC | Placement modifier fino | ACoS estável, TACoS <8% |
